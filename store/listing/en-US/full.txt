Blend Burst is a bright, relaxing match-3 puzzle game with a twist: colors mix! Swipe red into blue to make purple, blue into yellow to make green, and set off dazzling White Bursts when a color meets its missing partner.

★ MATCH, BLEND AND BURST
Match 3 or more tiles of the same color, or blend two primary colors into a brand new one. Secondary-color matches splash onto their neighbors for extra clears.

★ POWERFUL SPECIALS AND COMBOS
• Line Blaster – match 4 in a line or a 2×2 square to clear a whole row or column
• Paint Bomb – match in an L or T shape to blow up the area around it
• Prism – match 5 in a line to wipe out every tile of one color
• White Burst – blend a secondary color with its missing primary
Swipe two specials together for spectacular combos that can clear the entire board!

★ 120 LEVELS IN 8 PAINTED WORLDS
Travel from the Art Studio through the Rainbow Garden, Paint Factory, Sky Gallery, Ink Lagoon, Prism Peaks and Bucket Brigade all the way to Gravity Galaxy. Each world has its own look and music.

★ FRESH CHALLENGES
Clean paint stains, break glass, free chained tiles, stop spreading ink, deliver paint buckets and master gravity that shifts direction.

★ BOOSTERS, GIFTS AND EVENTS
• Brush, Free Swap, Color Splash and Gravity Flip boosters
• Start levels with a Line Blaster, Paint Bomb or Prism
• Daily gift calendar and win-streak rewards
• Weekly Paint Collector event and a 28-day season pass

★ PLAY YOUR WAY
• Optional Google Play Games sign-in for cloud save and leaderboards
• Colorblind-friendly tile shapes
• Optional reminders when your lives are full
• Plays in 15 languages, including yours
